<h3>Proposal Deadline: November 16, 2026</h3>

Regional soybean research collaborative programs are seeking collaborative multistate and multi-disciplinary soybean research project requests for funding to begin on January 1, 2027.  Funded projects may include both applied and basic research, and the communication of research results, to provide short- and long-term practical benefit to U.S. soybean producers.  Information about general guidelines, proposal requirements and submission process can be found on the <a href="https://data.soybase.org/annex/Glycine/max/documents/RFP/2026-2027_Multi-Regional_Soybean_Checkoff_RFP.pdf" target="_blank">full Research Request for Proposals</a>.
<br> <br>
Research proposals are expected to contain the provided <b>cover sheet, proposal overview and budget</b> (<a href="https://data.soybase.org/annex/Glycine/max/documents/RFP/2026-2027_Multi-Regional_Soybean_Checkoff_RFP_Template.pdf" target="_blank"> PDF Template</a> / <a href="https://data.soybase.org/annex/Glycine/max/documents/RFP/2026-2027_Multi-Regional_Soybean_Checkoff_RFP_MSWord_Template.docx" target="_blank">MS Word Template</a>). 
<br><br>
Projects must logically fit into one of these key research areas (all are equal in priority):
<ul>
  <li>Tools & Technology for Soybean Improvement</li>
  <li>Extreme Weather Resiliency</li>
  <li>Nationally Integrated Weed Management</li>
  <li>Deer Depredation: Uniting Research to Protect Soybeans</li>
  <li>Production-focused Practices or Innovations that Reduce Carbon Intensity Scores and/or Expand 
Access to New Markets</li>
  <li>Farmer Profitability and Economic Sustainability</li>
</ul>
